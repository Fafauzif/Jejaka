## Interpretation

Prewar photographs support architectural appearance at their depicted dates. Their use for November1945 is a continuity assumption unless corroborated. L005 is1948 and can only be used as a later comparison. Do not model cars, trams, signs, street furniture or damage as1945 facts solely because they appear in these views.

The contact sheet and crops reuse these same images and inherit the relevant attribution/share-alike conditions. The individual metadata files record image dimensions and SHA256 values.
