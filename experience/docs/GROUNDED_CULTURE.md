# Grounded revision: culture, dialogue and integration record

Production/source review: 31 October 2026, Asia/Jakarta. This file describes authored text and its integration requirements. A script edit is not evidence that the exchange was displayed, read, understood or retained in actual play.

The user chose real geographic relationships with disclosed distance compression, a four-to-five-minute main experience, and Indonesian captions plus environmental sound. There is no new voiceover, TTS or reused dialogue MP3 in this revision. Raka, Sari, Adi, Pak Wiryo and their words are fictional.

## Intended learning and claim boundaries

| Idea | Narrow claim | Proposed action/view | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tool | A canting carries hot batik wax for making dots/lines. | A distinct cup/spout and wax line at hand height; Raka helps hold the cloth. | It does not dispense the dye. No exact alloy, wax recipe or local workshop is authenticated. |
| Resist | Wax protects selected areas from dye during that colouring stage. | Compare a waxed pale area with an already prepared dyed example. | Pale lines are an example, not a rule that all batik has white lines. |
| Removal and stages | Wax can be removed with hot water, called nglorod; colour/wax stages may be repeated. | A separate prepared sample or restrained comparison of waxed and finished fabric. | Do not imply a whole cloth is manufactured in seconds. The exact recipe, temperature, dwell time and number of colours are not simulated. |
| Continuity | Knowledge is passed between people; losing a workplace/materials can interrupt practice. | The same teacher/child survive, keep some belongings, and discuss practising later at refuge. | Sari learning from her mother is personal fiction, not a claim that every batik maker learns within a family. One saved tool does not save all culture. |
| Historical setting | Independence did not mean the fighting immediately ended. | Adi's short question during a sheltered interval; opening and optional history provide the wider context. | Do not combine the September, October and November episodes or invent the exact attacker/weapon/date of a local blast. |

Sari is packing to leave an already troubled district. She shows previously prepared samples while Raka helps, rather than deciding to manufacture a complete cloth during an imminent bombardment. Her working place, pattern, belongings and family history are not documented Surabaya cases. Pattern art must remain original and unnamed as a local historical motif.

## Caption integration contract

Existing `LINES` and `STORY` keys are retained. New exchanges are data until the app calls them:

- `preparation`: 12.5 seconds; packing, role and immediate cooperation.
- `opening`: 15.5 seconds; canting, malam and safety around the hot tip.
- `clothDye`: 15 seconds; previously dyed example and resist principle.
- `clothFinish`: 23 seconds; removal, nglorod and possible repeated colouring.
- `shelteredHistory`: 16 seconds; independence and ongoing fighting.
- `shelteredMemory`: 20.5 seconds; materials/workspace left behind, without a declaration of total cultural destruction.
- `refugeRecall`: 18.5 seconds; Sari's fictional learning relationship and a possible next lesson for Adi.
- `finalIntact`: 18 seconds; retained tool, unfinished work left behind and rest.

The new `fact` field marks `canting`, `wax-resist`, `wax-removal`, `multiple-colours`, `independence-conflict` and `transmission` on particular lines. If used for observation, record a fact only after its caption has actually remained displayed for its duration. Enqueuing it is not presentation. It is never a human learning score.

Urgent danger calls must interrupt leisurely dialogue. Preserve or resume an interrupted cultural exchange in a suitable later safe moment; do not simply let a queued fact disappear and still credit it. Avoid playing both sheltered exchanges back-to-back while the player is exposed. The two refuge sequences may be placed across sitting, water and a final conversation; they must not run remotely while the family is still crossing.

The mapped crossing is intended to be Jembatan Merah, followed by the west-bank quay southward to the school area identified by the geography review. A school building can be map-supported while its use as this family's refuge remains fictional. Chapter coordinates are handed to the lead for the new layout; old canonical target values are not geographic evidence.

`STORY.pacing` gives design targets rather than compulsory idle gates. Normal length should come from useful movement, readable encounters, assistance and short safe pauses. Do not make the player wait for an invisible 240-second threshold. Fast traversal, skipped optional conversation, accessibility reading, pauses, loading and slow software rendering need distinct timing records.

The earlier app completed cloth assistance in 2.5 seconds, replaced opening dialogue on `learned`, and could issue its first warning at 22 seconds. Those unchanged mechanics cannot present the new sequence. The integration must coordinate visible stages, caption completion, actual input release, hazard priority and saved progress. The portable older browser drivers also assumed exactly four source links; there are now five. Verify required institutional URLs instead of treating a link count as a cultural contract.
