# Escape edition: cultural and historical claim record

Independent source/text review, 30 October 2026. This record separates supported background, authored civilian fiction, intended learning, and what still needs to be observed in the rendered experience. It is not evidence of human learning or a certificate of historical reconstruction.

## The learning focus

The small cultural focus is a technique: a canting places hot batik wax, called *malam*, on cloth; the wax resists the dye, leaving the protected area uncoloured by that dye bath. Merely showing a patterned fabric or a folding gesture does not demonstrate this mechanism. A visible before/after swatch should support the short spoken explanation. A naturally pale line may be an accessible demonstration; it must not imply that all batik always has white lines or that every colouring process consists of a single dip.

The second focus is transmission between people. Sari can teach Adi only if the people, knowledge and opportunity to practise continue. That relationship is personal fiction, not a claim that all batik makers are women or family-trained. A particular cloth does not stand for all Indonesian culture. The new geometric pattern is production art: it is not a named historical motif or an authenticated Surabaya pattern.

Helping should make cooperation visible. If Raka lifts a beam while Sari pulls Adi free, the reciprocal action communicates more than a label saying “gotong royong”. Likewise, moving a blocked cart should visibly open a crossing for other residents. These are fictional examples of cooperation, not claims about the documented conduct of a particular community during an identified attack.

## Historical context suitable for the experience

The setting can remain “Surabaya · November 1945”. A concise optional historical note can say that a major offensive began on 10 November and that Indonesian fighters faced British and Indian troops under British command. The surrounding conflict concerned Indonesian independence. Do not say that all fighting first began on 10 November, or label every opposing soldier Dutch.

One brief opening context caption could say: “Perang Dunia II telah berakhir. Di Surabaya, perjuangan mempertahankan kemerdekaan Indonesia masih berlangsung.” This is a paraphrase of the broad context supported by EH01/EH04, followed separately by the fictional group’s immediate escape goal. It does not establish the attack on this particular shop.

A quiet opening must read as a brief moment in an already troubled city, not proof that the whole city was peaceful before the first fictional blast. Preparing to leave or packing unfinished work, with distant fighting and neighbours closing stalls, would connect the ordinary cultural encounter to the escape without a sudden unexplained change of world.

The exact day, time, attacker position, weapon, weather, school, evacuation route and damage sequence are not established. The large new city layout is an authored playable district, not a surveyed reconstruction or a claim that a specific landmark burned. Sound, flame, debris, smoke and crowd choreography are production staging. A simulation or silent archival film cannot authenticate their appearance or sound.

## Authored story and checks

Raka, Sari, Adi, Pak Wiryo, their dialogue, family relationship, workshop, cloth, beam rescue, cart obstruction, refuge and survival outcomes are authored fiction. They are not recovered testimony. Replacing the old forced death with an escape ending is a narrative change responding to the user’s latest request for a coherent escape experience; it does not alter the historical outcome of the battle.

The first story-data draft was independently read before rendered integration. The following were requests, not completed visual tests at that point:

The draft already gives the player a role and destination, uses direct danger calls, and correctly describes the wax-resist principle. Its opening was shortened after review; the rescue verb was changed to match lifting the beam; regional address was simplified to “Mbak”; and Adi’s closing line was revised to recall the pale wax-protected line. These are text improvements. Their naturalness during actual play, caption timing, cultural legibility and consistency with animation require rendered review. No human visitor test, retention result or measured learning gain is claimed.

No institutional images, page layouts, film, source documents or extended quotations are copied into the runtime by this record. Source links do not confer asset licences. Existing research, source geometry and asset-specific attribution remain preserved separately.

## Integrated escape revision: observed scope

The final integrated text differs from the early draft described above. The workshop names the canting and malam while an original cloth illustration changes from pale fabric to indigo with pale wax-protected lines. At the refuge Adi asks whether Sari still has the canting; she says he can try later and asks him to sit. This connects the carried tool and surviving relationship to a future opportunity to practise. It does not claim that one rescued tool preserves an entire culture.

In the earlier frozen F portrait traversal, the working area, dye result, child release, bridge assistance and physical reunion were captured through ordinary native touch inputs. The same family reaches the refuge and sits before the closing exchange. Compact-phone captions were repositioned after they obscured the demonstration. These observations establish implemented staging, not visitor comprehension. A separate fresh, unaided UI critic is recorded in `ESCAPE_VERIFICATION.md`; it remains an AI legibility check rather than a human learning study.

The drying rack is a grounded production prop; the rescue animation uses lifting, crawling and standing. The practical reach tolerance is deliberately larger than an anatomical arm's reach. There is no contact-solved player hand model or continuous handholding simulation. The spoken instructions were revised to “stay near” rather than claiming a handhold the animation does not perform.
