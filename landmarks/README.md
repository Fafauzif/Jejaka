The downloaded images are references, not finished textures or a model. Five image files, four crops and a contact sheet are included. They were visually inspected. No image was generated by AI. The dates range from circa 1910 to 1948 and must remain visible in interpretation.

Do not place the current governor's clocktower building at the former residency site beside the bridge. The official municipal governor-office record distinguishes the previous office near the bridge from the new office completed 1931 at Jl.Pahlawan110. The continued existence, form and use of the former bridge-side office in 1945 need separate verification. Likewise, an old photograph labelled residentshuis is not enough to certify its 1945 survival.

A map's black building symbol needs identity confirmation. If the index says P.W.D Offices/Burg Genesk Dienst, do not rename it Internatio because it is near the expected area. Keep uncertain location candidates editable and report the mismatch.
