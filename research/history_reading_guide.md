# Reading and acquisition guide

## Highest-value already-inspected sources

- **H002, P. R. S. Mani dispatches and diary:** contemporary source with strong chronological and perspective value. Start with the exact PDF page locators in `history_claims.json`. Keep the author's military/public-relations role explicit. Read scans when OCR is consequential.
- **H003, IWM JFU 437 catalogue:** discovery guide to period visual material and to uncertain place identifications. Its metadata is inspected; the film itself is not bundled or claimed as viewed in this history task.
- **H011, Basundoro and Sofansyah (2024):** municipal place-history guide with useful photo captions and source trails. Inspect chapter and image dates, not just the title. Its older and modern images cannot all depict 1945. A conflict is logged in `history_source_criticism.md`.
- **H012, Wisnu, Sumarno and Patridina:** scholarly full-text methodological reading. The article landing page supplies publication and issue metadata. Keep the difference between observed visual content and inferred interpretation.

## Further reading with explicit access limits

- **H009:** published review of Nugroho Notosusanto et al., *Pertempuran Surabaya*, second edition 1995. The review was inspected. The book was not read in this task. Follow it as a bibliography lead rather than saying the kit contains the book's findings.
- **H013:** Francis Palmos, UWA doctoral thesis, 2011. Repository metadata inspected; the PDF endpoint returned 403. The repository provides a specific personal-study condition. No full-text claims or reproduction rights are assumed.
- **H014:** Vilomena Theorina HB., Sanata Dharma skripsi, 2007. Full text is listed but repeated retrieval was unreliable. Its institutional record is useful to locate the thesis. Content is unverified here.

## Acquisition status must persist

When a later agent successfully retrieves a source:

1. Update its access and inspection status with the retrieval date.
2. Record original file identity and any rights statement.
3. Inspect only the relevant section first.
4. Add a claim with exact page or frame locator.
5. Link that claim to an asset or scene decision.
6. Record contradictory evidence before changing geometry.

Do not keep retrying the same failed endpoint indefinitely. Use an institutional alternate viewer or mark the reference inaccessible. No external contact has been initiated by this package.

## Rights and delivery

Publicly viewable does not mean unrestricted asset reuse. The complete primary PDFs and article images have not been copied into the ZIP as reusable textures. The original summaries, ledgers and modelling procedures in this folder are authored for the reconstruction kit. Underlying source rights remain with their owners.

If the user later wishes to publish an archival image inside the finished product, independently verify the exact item's terms. Research consultation and final-product redistribution are different uses.
