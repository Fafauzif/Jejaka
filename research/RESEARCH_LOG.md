# Research boundaries and retrieval record

Prepared2026-10-23. This records the bounded preparation run, not an exhaustive archive search or a claim that every available source was found.

## Decisions established before acquisition
Scope: district-scale reconstruction around Jembatan Merah/Kali Mas. Target period: November1945. Required subjects: cartographic base, landmark appearance/identity, dated context, source access and reuse conditions. Source priority: original records/images, institutional metadata and heritage records, then scholarly interpretation. Production assets are separate from historical evidence.

## Search families actually used
- Soerabaja/Surabaya/Sourabaya wartime maps,1943cityplan, NEFIS townplan, HIND1072, D_E_57_8.
- BritishLibrary/Leiden/KITLV/AustralianWarMemorial map holdings and source links.
- Internatio Heerenstraat Willemsplein, RoodeBrug, GedungSinga/Algemeene, KembangDjepoen period images and institutional records.
- Surabaya1945dispatches, IWMJFU437, Mani, Indonesian museum/ANRI chronology and urban photo research.
- Official Blender5.2/OpenCode compaction/skills documentation and pinned Blender skills repository.
- Primary CC0 asset/material libraries for modest local reusable models and PBR maps.

## Acquisition results
The full M001image was acquired from its public-domain-designated Commons source after an initial directURL403; a download query and declared research user-agent succeeded. The original7452×10970file was retained. Institutional mapcatalogues M002–M004were inspected; their separate scans were not obtained. Duplicated/temporary map downloads were not needed in the deliverable.

Five selected photograph files were downloaded with per-image metadata and licence records. Reference crops and a board were created without AI enhancement/colourisation. Several institutional images and periodfilm remain external because their usage status or acquisition path was unsuitable for redistribution.

The Mani49pagePDF was acquired for internal research, with selected pages inspected and exact locators recorded. It was left out of the distributable archive because its repository usage wording is qualified. Scholarly/photo research, heritage records and later municipal publications were inspected to support interpretation and expose contradictions; inaccessible thesis fulltexts remain bibliography leads only.

Official OpenCode documentation and Blender repository source informed the helper workflow. Some exact Blender5.2API pages returned access errors in the preparation environment; local introspection remains required. The pinned Blender skill installer was inspected but not executed on the owner's machine.

Selected production meshes, texture files and licences were acquired. Source hashes, image decoding and model dependency checks are recorded in `assets/ASSET_VERIFICATION.md` and the asset checks JSON. They do not establish historical presence.

## Stopping rule
Stop when the district has usable cartographic evidence, supported appearance briefs and a clear record of unknowns, with runtime helpers validated as far as the available environment allows. Do not extend the scope into fullcity/fullcampaign coverage merely to increase source count. Further sources should close named gaps rather than repeat already established claims.
