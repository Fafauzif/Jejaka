## How to interpret the references

The user asks for the quality of art direction at a feasible lower-poly fidelity. A simple mesh with an intentional silhouette, well-composed light and credible motion is useful; a photorealistic mesh in an empty flat-lit street does not meet the goal. Production should create a consistent visual language across sky, architecture, props, people, fire, smoke, UI and sound.

Cloverfield informs an embodied, incomplete civilian view and sudden collective reaction. Motion should follow movement and impact, with a reduced-motion alternative. Continuous random shaking or unreadable camera tilt is not required to achieve that atmosphere. Camera hardware, modern cars and contemporary street clothing in a supplied reference do not become appropriate historical assets.

Expedition 33 informs palette discipline, character readability, mood and scene composition. The actual costumes, fantasy objects, creatures, weapons and story incidents in those screenshots are not requested Jejaka content. Create original assets suited to the Surabaya premise and verify any specific historical detail before treating it as evidence.
