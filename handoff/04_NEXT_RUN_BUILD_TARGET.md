# Next-run build target: a living city and its endangered memory

This is a suggested execution shape derived from the locked answers, not a newly researched historical account or a rigid screenplay. The next agent should improve it where the actual code, geometry, research and rendered play reveal a better solution. Do not ask a fresh preference questionnaire.

## Start with the product gap

The checkpoint is a functioning browser slice with free movement, instanced civilians, effects, branching notes and save/export. Its passing functional tests do not establish the user's visual, mobile, cultural-learning or three-minute goals. The next run must revise the product around those goals rather than add another layer of menus to the current errand.

The key change is from "document things, then read the explanation" to "move among people, help preserve something they value, and understand the loss by witnessing the before and after."

## Approximately 180 seconds of ordinary play

| Approximate play time | Player activity | City/war staging | Cultural function |

| 0–25 s | Enter a busy street, orient while already able to move, encounter one resident with a short practical request | Several simultaneous routines, distinct near/mid/far people, a bridge/river orientation, distant pressure | Show a specific sourced local practice or relationship through activity and a short understandable line. |
| 25–65 s | Move through cover toward a person or practical task, choose one of two nearby paths | Warning crosses the crowd before the nearer disruption; shutters, people and belongings react differently | Establish why a person, object, craft, meeting place or record matters before asking the player to save it. |

| 120–160 s | Traverse the altered return lane, observe a consequence, preserve the account | Recognizable earlier street now changed; civilians remain actors, not only fleeing decorations | The before/after makes cultural loss concrete. Record only what the fictional journalist can know. |
| 160–180 s | Short notebook handoff and fixed non-graphic ending, followed by a concise optional context view | Restrain the final composition enough to read a human action amid continuing chaos | End with what survived through people and the notebook, not only a slogan or a count of destroyed buildings. |

This timing is a production target to validate through real play. Pause and optional reading do not require a strict deadline. Eliminate empty travel and mandatory long text holds. Do not halve real-time movement merely to control the story. Keep at least one meaningful navigation decision and one useful helping action.

## Cultural content must be researched and playable

Choose a small coherent set of cultural details rather than an encyclopedic list. Possible production directions include a neighborhood exchange, a local skill, written community/business memory, or people helping one another. These are candidate story devices pending source verification, not assertions about a particular historical shop, family or event.

For each chosen element record: exact claim; supporting source and what it actually supports; period/local applicability; fictional completion; where the player sees/hears/does it; and how its survival/loss changes the final account. If evidence is weak, use a modest fictional personal value without representing it as a verified cultural fact. Do not flatten a diverse city into one costume, stereotype, prop, religious identity or generic "tradition."

At least one thing should be understood by watching an ordinary action before the impact. At least one thing should be learned through helping, not by opening a history modal. The historical afterword can clarify evidence boundaries and give sources; it cannot be the only place culture appears.

## Concrete implementation priorities

1. **Repair the checkpoint seams first.** Re-render the final sky fix. Align Damar's objective marker with his live interaction anchor. Check the two-impact restoration state and source visibility. Keep restored choices/photos and the no-posthumous-writing contract correct.
2. **Make mobile a complete input path.** The current pad is a starting point, not proof of full support. Provide comfortable simultaneous touch look/movement, contextual help, crouch/cover, pause, camera/notebook if retained, and reliable pointer cancellation. Account for safe areas and both orientations. No core step may depend on hover, pointer lock, Ctrl, Shift, E or Space.
3. **Re-cut the route to three minutes.** Combine/reduce objectives and shorten lines/physical distance. Update rules, UI, saves/versioning, audio/captions and tests together. Do not preserve nine entries or three modal choices as an arbitrary technical requirement if they obstruct the revised flow; retain evidence integrity and choices that matter.
4. **Art-direct one inhabited street.** Coherent materials and lighting, silhouettes with variation, interiors that imply occupation, objects at human scale, and camera compositions with layered people. Avoid uniform flat facades and repeated bodies. Original lower-poly art is acceptable when it looks intentional and moves convincingly.
5. **Make helping visible.** Move/guide/lift/open through a short responsive action with a world consequence. Show who benefits or what is preserved. Use an animation that fits the available asset quality; do not label a modal flag change a contact-solved rescue.
6. **Make cover useful and readable.** Directional danger, shelter geometry, understandable movement windows and recoverable local consequences. The story continues after mistakes. Avoid invisible punishment or random mandatory restart loops.
7. **Stage chaotic war as a shared event.** Anticipation, crossing cue/source, impact, human reactions, debris/dust, fire/light and changed routes must belong to one sequence. Vary reaction delays and destinations. Spend mobile effects budgets on the layers that explain cause/effect.
8. **Default to Indonesian.** Short practical speech/captions; all important control labels in Indonesian. Match any reused audio to the actual line and scene. Add new original/free audio only with known rights and reasonable local delivery.
9. **Optimize what is actually costly.** Profile scene submission, transparency, shadows, pixel ratio, civilian update tiers and memory in worst-case views. Tune spatial batching, far representation and quality tiers based on evidence. Full asset streaming is optional if the compact resident slice does not need it.
10. **Run independent critique/fix/recheck rounds.** Separate technical behavior from subjective visual taste. Fix the largest player-visible defects before adding map size or long documents.
